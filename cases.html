01Retail bank construction

The Element Group

The situation

Four years after start-up, this retail bank construction firm was profitable and had recouped part of its start-up costs. Leadership wanted scale, and sought help in three areas: improving loyalty and repeat rate; developing recurring-revenue lines, since 100% of revenue came from highly cyclical capital projects; and building brand equity as a commercial real estate specialist.

The work

A situation analysis with qualitative and quantitative studies drove three years of execution: keep winning design-and-build customers, while leveraging TEG's real estate and branding expertise as new lines for current and lapsed ones. The firm was positioned as a trusted full-lifecycle branch partner across:

  • Customer experience — merchandising and marketing
  • Consumer insight leadership
  • Real estate guidance — site selection and development
  • Branch fulfilment — the low-cost partner for all customer branches

In the seven years since

  • Revenue has quadrupled
  • Several large regional banks count TEG as agency of record, with 100% share of wallet
  • Annual customer awareness and attitude insight has improved brand awareness and industry credibility
02Top-20 US grocery chain

Giant Eagle

The situation

Giant Eagle was losing share as consumers shifted spend toward value players such as Aldi and Walmart. Private label was financially material — 13% of sales, 27% of gross margin — but lagged competitors in contribution and growth, managed internally as a strategic afterthought by category managers. The portfolio had no coherence: 14 brands, 5,000-plus SKUs, 314 categories, down to and including firewood.

The work

Our principal led the pursuit of relevance, and eventual fame, for the exclusive brands. Alignment came first, behind a strategy of focus, simplification and consistency. New capabilities were built in product management, development and packaging engineering — replicating a leading FMCG firm's approach while leveraging the data and merchandising priority only a retailer has.

Two emerging brands were built as differentiators: Market District for indulgent and foodie-focused, Nature's Basket for natural and organic.

Results

  • Share of chain sales +1.5 points per year over three years; +41% top line
  • Gross margin improved 25 bps per year
  • Annual product launches doubled; cycle time cut 50%
  • Two new brands grown to $150M in three years
  • Brands consolidated 14 → 5; exited 7 non-strategic brands and 30 categories
  • SKU count reduced 35–40% over three years
03High-performance tires

Goodyear

The situation

Goodyear was losing share in high-performance tires. All three brands — Goodyear, Dunlop and Kelly — were decaying from a lack of news and investment, and Goodyear Eagle had lost its leadership position to Michelin through simple atrophy.

Flawed segmentation models prevented any clean read of the category, and while customer relationships were strong, the company was out of touch with high-performance consumer attitudes. R&D was not returning an acceptable ROI.

The work

Five organisational needs framed the engagement:

  • An accurate market segmentation, to drive alignment
  • Consolidation of resources pursuing competing agendas
  • That segmentation turned into a portfolio innovation strategy
  • Opportunities and threats ranked by probability and impact
  • A codified three-year roadmap with organisational support

Results

  • Three new lines launched in year one of the roadmap
  • $150M in year-one revenue from those launches
04Close, inventory and variance reporting

Automation in the finance function

The situation

A month-end close assembled largely by hand: source exports pulled and stitched together, sub-ledgers tied to the GL manually, and the reporting pack rebuilt from scratch whenever a late entry landed.

The work

A refreshable query layer replaced the manual stitching, scripted reconciliation with exception flags replaced the tie-out, and templated outputs bound to the query layer replaced the rebuild. The same plumbing then produced two logistics reports — an inventory masterfile dashboard surfacing revenue at risk, and line-level variance with thresholds that surface only the movements worth explaining.

Results

  • Close cut from 5 days to 3 — a 40% time reduction
  • Report preparation cut from roughly 3,600 minutes to 2
  • Tooling still in use across finance, logistics and planning after handover
