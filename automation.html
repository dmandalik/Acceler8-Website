05Case study

Inventory and variance reporting.

Two logistics reports out of the same plumbing as the close work, at a fraction of the cost of the first build.

Inventory masterfile

The storage guide already held age, location and movement for every item; nothing new had to be collected. We built a company-wide dashboard on top of it that surfaced orders yet to ship — revealing revenue at risk and the shipments to move out first. It stayed in use across finance, logistics and planning long after the engagement ended.

Line-level variance

Period-over-period and budget-to-actual variance computed on every line, with thresholds that surface only the movements worth explaining to management. The reporting team was trained on the tooling directly, so the time saving carried forward without us.
