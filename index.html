03Business challenges

Four things stall an AI programme.

  1. The data isn't ready

    Inconsistent, scattered or ungoverned data stops most initiatives before they start — whichever process you point them at.

  2. The pilot never scales

    A proof of concept works once, for one team, on one data set, and there is no route from there to something the business runs on.

  3. There is no strategy to align to

    Without an agreed direction, teams pursue competing agendas and the budget is spread thin across initiatives that never compound.

  4. Nobody owns it after handover

    The capability sits with whoever built it. When they move on, the work quietly reverts to being done by hand.
