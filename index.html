03Business challenges

Four things stall an AI programme.

  1. Getting the data right

    Inconsistent, scattered or ungoverned data stops most initiatives before they start.

  2. Getting a pilot to scale

    A January 2026 Deloitte study found that 7% of AI initiatives at participating firms were being scaled.

  3. No AI strategy at all

    38% of respondents indicated their company had no AI strategy to align to.

  4. A shortage of AI talent

    Companies attribute a slow start to a lack of AI talent.
