02What we are building

A strategy is only real once everyone can say it out loud.

Most plans fail in the gap between the deck and the organisation.

A winning strategy is built on market and customer insight, and beats the competition by design rather than by accident. But the test of whether you have one is simpler: can someone in field sales or shipping say how the company intends to win, and what their part in it is?

If they can, work gets prioritised the same way in every team. If they cannot, you have a document, not a direction. We run both axes at once — external competitiveness, so the plan is worth following, and internal collaboration, so it is followed.

Blockers we plan around

  • Unclear direction
  • Data inconsistencies
  • Legacy systems and processes
  • AI answer integrity
  • Training requirements
  • Privacy and ethics concerns

We help clients document, communicate and execute a strategy that holds up once it meets the organisation.

03Engagement

How the strategy gets built.

01

Learn the business, the customer and the competitor

We interview leadership and the commercial team, read the market, and map the customer journey end to end — with particular attention to the moments of truth, where a customer decides whether you are worth staying with.

Output
Situation analysis, customer journey map, competitive read
Who
Leadership, commercial and operations leads
02

Generate candidate initiatives with the business team

Structured sessions with your people, grounded in what discovery found rather than run in a workshop vacuum. We push toward ideas that change the game instead of defending the current one.

Output
Candidate initiative long-list, with the reasoning attached
Who
Cross-functional working group
03

Size every idea on value and on probability

Each candidate is scored twice: the value it could create, and the likelihood it lands given your data and capability today. Scorecards settle what would otherwise go to whoever argues hardest.

Output
Scored initiative register, and the method so you can re-run it
Who
Finance and the named initiative owners
04

Rank on potential and feasibility, then sequence it

Ideas are ranked and staged into a roadmap with owners and dates — sequenced so early work builds the data and capability the later work depends on. Sequencing is where most of the value is won or lost.

Output
Three-year roadmap, owner map, staging plan
Who
Leadership team
05

Codify it so the organisation can articulate it

We turn the roadmap into a narrative the business can repeat, brief the teams who have to act on it, and set the cadence for reviewing it. Then we stay on, because the plan will meet reality and need adjusting.

Output
Strategy narrative, communications pack, review cadence
Who
The whole organisation
